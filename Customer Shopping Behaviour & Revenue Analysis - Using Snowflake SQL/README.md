# Customer Shopping Behaviour & Revenue Analysis

### Project Overview
Customer Shopping Behaviour & Revenue Analysis is a data analytics project built using Snowflake and SQL to analyze customer transactions, purchasing patterns, revenue performance, product categories, brands, locations, payment methods, online stores, gender, age groups, and monthly sales trends.

The project transforms raw customer shopping transaction data into meaningful business insights that can help an e-commerce/retail business understand customer behaviour, identify high-performing products and locations, and monitor revenue performance

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Snowflake – Cloud data warehouse

SQL – Data analysis and business insights

CSV – Source data

### 📊 Analysis Performed

•	Total Revenue Analysis

•	Monthly Revenue Trend

•	Revenue by Product Category

•	Top 5 Brands by Order Volume

•	Top 5 Locations by Revenue

•	Shopping Behavior by Gender

•	Customer Shopping by Age Group

•	Payment Method Analysis

•	Online Store Performance

### 📌 Key Results

•	Total Revenue: ₹64.68M

•	Highest Revenue Category: Footwear

•	Highest Order Volume Brand: Max

•	Highest Revenue Location: Lucknow

•	Leading Payment Method: UPI
